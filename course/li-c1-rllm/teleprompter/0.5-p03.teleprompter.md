### Beat 1 — talk (107 words)

Picture a tool that nudges students to copy answers. It makes no headline. Skills erode a little at a time, and later teachers and employers carry the gap. A slow cost is still a cost. Some costs arrive late. Cost-cutting today can become a legal bill tomorrow. If a flagged person sues, the company pays later. The externality returns as a liability. Check each feature against four groups: users, non-users, workers, the public. If someone outside the deal bears a cost they did not agree to, you found an externality. Add the third column. For every feature, ask who bears the cost, even if nobody meant it.
