### Beat 1 — talk (102 words)

Before we pick a model, follow the bytes. With a hosted API, the packet leaves your machine. It crosses your network and lands in a provider's data center. Open that packet. Your question, plus everything you pasted with it. A log file. A customer email. A config with a key in it. The provider receives all of it as text. Two questions follow. Residency: which country holds your text? Retention: how long does a copy live? Our client only sends text and reads a reply. It reports neither answer. Read the provider's terms. Cost moves too. A hosted API bills per token.
