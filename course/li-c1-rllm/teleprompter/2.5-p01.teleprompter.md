### Beat 1 — talk (103 words)

Two models. One prompt. Which answer is better? That is a match. Public leaderboards rank models this way, from many pairwise votes. Elo gives each model one rating. Everyone starts at 1000. K sets how far one match can move a rating. Ours is 32. Before a match, predict it. The expected score of A over B depends on the rating gap. The two expected scores sum to one. Equal ratings give one half. After the match, update. An underdog who wins moves a lot. A favorite who wins moves little. Our demo plays 18 matches. Six cases sit in one data file.
