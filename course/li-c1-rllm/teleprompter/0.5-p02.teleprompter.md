### Beat 1 — talk (109 words)

Someone else clones a voice to scam a family. Who pays? The reader who followed false advice. The family that lost savings. The platform that must moderate. None were customers. This is not only about big vendors. Deploy a model in your app and you can create externalities. A chatbot whose wrong guidance a user passes to others. A classifier that wrongly flags a person. An agent that emails the wrong list. Some costs are physical. Data centers use electricity. In 2024 they used about 415 terawatt-hours, roughly 1.5 percent of world electricity. That counts all workloads, not only AI. Ask who carries that load. Some costs are slow.
