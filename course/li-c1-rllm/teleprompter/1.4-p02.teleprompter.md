### Beat 1 — talk (106 words)

Hardware divided by lifetime. That is what the machine costs each month, used or idle. Power is extra. Second, energy per million tokens. One million tokens divided by your speed gives seconds. Seconds times watts gives joules. Convert joules to kilowatt-hours. Times your power price, that gives dollars. Local cost is the fixed cost plus energy times volume. Hosted cost is price times volume. Break-even is where they cross. It is the fixed cost divided by a gap. The gap is the hosted price minus your energy cost per million tokens.
