### Beat 1 — talk (109 words)

When a worker loses income to a model, the bill can land on society: retraining, support, lost skills. The company keeps the revenue. Name three groups. Takers use the pool. Makers refill it. Bystanders pay without choosing to. Inside the makers, look at the small creator. A large publisher can negotiate a license. A lone illustrator has little leverage. Your rules either protect the weak side or leave it to absorb the loss. Real commons can survive with rules. Elinor Ostrom studied fisheries, forests and irrigation systems. Users there help set and change the rules. AI has the same tools. Licenses. Opt-outs. Revenue sharing. Provenance records. None fixes everything.
