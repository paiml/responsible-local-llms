### Beat 1 — talk (103 words)

Start with one question. When value is created, who keeps it? Here is a spectrum. It maps onto AI. First, the free rider. A free rider uses something and pays nothing. Picture a public radio station. Many listen. Few give. If enough listeners free ride, the station goes dark. Software works the same way. If everyone copies a tool and nobody pays, the developer stops building it. Next, the rent seeker. A rent seeker takes a cut and adds little. A gate with a worker adds work. A gate with no one in it just stands in the way. Then the partner model.
