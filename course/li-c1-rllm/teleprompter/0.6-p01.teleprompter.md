### Beat 1 — talk (102 words)

Tech has a pattern called regulatory entrepreneurship. Elizabeth Pollman and Jordan Barry described it in 2017. Build a business in a legal gray area. Then work to change the law. The playbook has steps. Find a gray area. Grow fast, until the service is too big to ban. Mobilize users, so voters push your side. The playbook aims at state and local rules. The cases the paper studied focus there. You have seen it. Uber, Airbnb, Tesla and DraftKings are cases they studied. Each case raises one question: who pays while the law catches up? Watch for this pattern in generative AI.
