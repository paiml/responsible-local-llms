### Beat 1 — talk (107 words)

The question is what happens at scale. One person skipping a payment is invisible. A million people skipping it can end the thing they depend on. Take open-weight models. A lab pays to train one and gives it away. Everyone who downloads it rides free on that spending. Ask what keeps the lab funded. That answer sets how long the gift lasts. A platform sits between you and your customers. It can change the terms. It can raise a fee, change a ranking, or copy your best product. Sellers can only react. Map this to AI. A hosted model API you cannot leave looks like a platform.
