### Beat 1 — talk (107 words)

Start with a small town and its restaurants. The food and prices are alike. Anyone can open a new place. Economists call this close to perfect competition. In perfect competition, economic profit trends to zero in the long run. Economic profit is what you earn above a normal return. New entrants keep arriving until the extra profit is gone. Add a talented chef. The chef is scarce. Restaurants bid for the chef, and the salary rises. Soon the chef captures the profit. A restaurant that overpays can go bankrupt. The rule: profit goes to whoever holds the scarce resource. If nothing is scarce, nobody keeps extra profit.
