### Beat 1 — talk (103 words)

Picture a public pasture. Anyone can graze cattle there. No one owns it. This sets up the tragedy of the commons. Each rancher reasons the same way. If I don't add cattle, someone else will. So everyone adds cattle. The grass thins. Then it is gone. Everyone loses. Swap the pasture for human creative work. Books, photos, code, music, articles. People made them because the work paid, in money or recognition. Generative models graze on that work. One AI company downloaded pirated books. In July 2026 a court approved a 1.5 billion dollar settlement with the authors. The settlement covered the pirated downloads.
