### Beat 1 — talk (108 words)

Game theory studies choices when your outcome depends on someone else's choice. There are players, strategies and payoffs. The classic case is the prisoner's dilemma. Two suspects are held apart. Both stay quiet: one year each. Both confess: five years each. One confesses and one stays quiet: the talker walks, the other gets twenty. These are illustrative numbers. Take one seat. Whatever the other does, confessing pays you more. So both confess. Both get five years. Silence would have given each of them one. That is a dominant strategy with a worse result. Put two AI labs in the matrix. Each can test for safety or ship first.
