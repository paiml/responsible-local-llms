### Beat 1 — talk (108 words)

Law and guidance agree on one idea. Collect and keep only what you need. GDPR calls it data minimisation: adequate, relevant, limited to what is necessary. OWASP's list for language models names the risk: sensitive information disclosure. OWASP advises: pattern matching can detect and redact confidential content before processing. Our detector is small. Plain byte scanning. No regex engine. No model. No network. Six kinds. Email. SSN. Card. Phone. IP address. API key. Each has a placeholder in square brackets. Rules, not guesses. A card runs 13 to 19 digits. It must pass the Luhn checksum. An SSN never starts with 000, 666 or 9. Those are skipped.
