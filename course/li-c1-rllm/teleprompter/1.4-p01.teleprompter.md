### Beat 1 — talk (110 words)

Two ways to pay for a model. Hosted: a price per million tokens. Local: buy a machine, then pay for power. Find where the two lines cross. The demo reads its inputs from economics dot rs. The example machine costs 2,400 dollars over 36 months. It draws 250 watts at 15 cents a kilowatt-hour. The example hosted price is 3 dollars per million tokens. These are example inputs, fixed in the code. One input is measured, not typed: tokens per second. With RLL_LIVE set to 1, the demo makes a real call and times it. Offline, it uses the wall time saved with a stored reply. First, the fixed cost.
