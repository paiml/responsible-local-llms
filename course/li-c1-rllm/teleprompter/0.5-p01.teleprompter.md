Companies track profit and loss. Most spreadsheets leave out a third column. It holds costs that land on people outside the deal. Economists call it a negative externality. Take an extreme case. A criminal group offers to dispose of a company's waste for a fee. It dumps the waste at the edge of a small town. It keeps the money. The company got rid of its waste. The group got paid. The town got ruined land and the whole bill. Now AI. Say a vendor sells a text and voice product and books the revenue. Someone uses it to flood the web with false health advice.
