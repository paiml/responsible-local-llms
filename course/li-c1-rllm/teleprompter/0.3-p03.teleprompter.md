### Beat 1 — talk (98 words)

Each lab can mean well and still land here. The payoffs push them. Moral appeals do not change payoffs. Take a lab that wants to test carefully. It fears a rival will ship first and take its customers. Its safety team loses the argument to its sales team, quarter after quarter. The way out is to change the payoffs. Make skipping safety cost something. A customer who demands test results. A procurement rule. A regulation. A public leaderboard. Visibility matters in a repeated game. Open tests and shared reports let players see who defects. Repetition can hold cooperation.
