Two sides split revenue by bargaining, and each can walk away. In the NBA, a union contract sets the split. Players get 49 to 51 percent of basketball income. UFC fighters have received under 20 percent of revenue. Now the platform. A platform takes a fee on every sale between buyers and sellers. Sellers compete. Margins shrink. The platform collects either way. Last, the direct seller. No middleman. You keep the whole share. You also find every customer yourself. These are not villains and heroes. A free rider can be a student with no budget. A platform can give small sellers a real market. Each role has a use.
