### Beat 1 — talk (108 words)

Your data. Your workflow. Your tests. A competitor cannot download them. Keep the model swappable. Changing it should take one line. Some things stay scarce even when models are close. Domain knowledge. Customer trust. Treat the model as a utility, like electricity. You need it. You pay for it. Nobody picks a restaurant for its power company. The value is in what you cook. Commodity does not mean worthless. Electricity is a commodity, and whole industries run on it. The margin lives elsewhere. Spend your effort there. Local models help. If you can run the task on your own hardware, you have a fallback and a price check.
