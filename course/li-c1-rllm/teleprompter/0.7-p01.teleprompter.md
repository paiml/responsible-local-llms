### Beat 1 — talk (105 words)

Bias in AI has two parts. Creating it and reinforcing it. They need different fixes. First, creation. Say your dataset holds stereotypes and hate. The model learns the patterns and generates more of them. The bias came in with the data. Now reinforcement. Feed that content to a recommender. If harmful content earns more clicks, the engine learns that harm earns engagement. Ranking by engagement amplifies divisive posts. A 2023 Twitter experiment with 806 users showed this. In another Twitter study, each moral-emotional word raised sharing about 20 percent. Follow the loop. Biased data trains the model. The model makes content. Clicks rank the content.
