### Beat 1 — talk (109 words)

The lesson 6 demo calls it, with the minimum at 1.0: every case passes every seed. A real model is another matter. Real-runtime checks do not sit in the pull-request gate. The workflow calls them a lab check. They run in a nightly workflow named live. One job, on the gx10 runner, starts llama.cpp and Ollama from pinned images, and apr from the runner's path. It cannot block a merge or a release, so it never runs on a pull request. It runs at 3:17 UTC each night, or on demand. The script behind it runs the chapter 1, chapter 2 and chapter 4 runtime tests against each runtime.
